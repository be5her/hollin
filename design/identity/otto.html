Identity · 03

Otto, in every state

The assistant as it appears on Craft's paper: the mark, the chat panel in each of its states, the canvas halo and tag, History, the status bar, Home, the empty project and Settings. Light on the left, dark on the right. Every colour is a variable from Craft §e or decisions §4; mulberry never asks you to click it, except Draft it.

The mark

A mulberry tile (--ai) with Otto's room on it: a paper-coloured outlined square (--ai-fg) and the pen, a dot, still on its top-right corner. 28 px in the panel header, 24 on the Home composer, 20 on the empty-project line and in Settings, 16 in History rows, 14 on card corners and in the call log. Tile radius 7 / 6 / 5 / 4 / 3.5 px; the room's stroke is 2 px at 24 and up, 2.4 px below so it survives. The glyph without its tile is what the canvas tag carries.

The chat panel

418 px wide, the right column of the project tab. The header carries the mark, Otto in Fraunces, the model in mono and the standing rule. Your messages sit on the elevated paper, right-aligned; Otto's are paragraphs with a 2 px mulberry rule on the left. Tool calls are paper cards with a 3 px mulberry left edge. Every deciding button is yours: terracotta or paper.

The canvas

What Otto changed in the last minute carries a 34 px halo of --plan-ai at 22 % alpha with round end nodes and one tag on a short leader; a pending destructive call draws its target with a dashed outline until you answer. Here the kitchen is selected by you in terracotta at the same time, a moss snap mark sits on a corner, and the dimension stays indigo. Sizes are screen pixels and do not follow the zoom.

History

Newest first. Your rows have a graphite dot, Otto's the 16 px mark on --ai-tint and a signature with layer and command id, MCP agents' a hollow ring and no tint. The newest entry carries a 2 px terracotta bar: it is the one Ctrl+Z takes. A call waiting for your yes sits greyed at the top and is not yet an undo step.

The status bar

Between the units and MCP: a mulberry dot and a short state. The dot pulses while Otto works, goes graphite when Otto cannot work, danger for six seconds after a failed run. The button around it is paper; clicking it opens the Assistant tab.

Home

The first slot is the composer card on --ai-tint with the one mulberry button in the app, Draft it. While Otto drafts, the card shows the sentence and a spinner; without a key the field is disabled and the button is a paper Set up Otto. A project Otto has worked on carries the 14 px mark in its thumbnail's corner.

The empty project

Concept 1's card, plus the line on --ai-tint: "Or tell Otto:" with an editable example sentence; Enter sends it as the first message. First launch carries the same line under New project and Import.

Settings and About

Settings > Assistant names the provider, explains the rule, and holds the key or the Claude Code command and its status. The MCP call log attributes Otto's calls with the 14 px mark and other agents' with a hollow ring, untinted. About has the one two-colour credit line.

Otto's mark

A mulberry rounded tile with Otto's room on it: a paper-coloured outlined square with the pen, a dot, on its top-right corner (otto-mark.svg). 28 px in the panel header, 24 and 20 in excerpts and tags, 16 in History rows, 14 on Home cards. No face, no sparkle; mulberry never asks you to click it.

Type

Three OFL families, bundled as variable files. Alegreya Sans is the working face; IBM Plex Mono carries every number, unit, id and key cap; Fraunces speaks only in the wordmark, panel and dialog titles, and the Home and empty-moment headings. The tinted rows are Otto's typographic treatment.

Palette

Every semantic variable of styles.css, both themes: Craft's tables plus the five Otto variables (--ai, --ai-hover, --ai-fg, --ai-tint, --plan-ai). The swatches read their hex from this page's stylesheet, so they cannot drift from the CSS above. Mulberry is the only magenta-family colour in the app: 17° terracotta, 42° ochre, 85° sage, 140° moss, 228° indigo, 325° mulberry.

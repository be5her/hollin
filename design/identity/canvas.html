The drawings · plan, section, 3D, thumbnails, sheets

What the app draws, in the identity: the plan canvas with everything it can show at once, the section, the 3D view by day and night in both themes, a Home card at its real size, an A3 sheet with the proposed title block and the DXF layer colours. Hex values are read back from this page's own CSS variables, which repeat Craft's tables and the decisions' Otto row verbatim; 04-canvas.md is the specification.

(a) The plan canvas · 15 × 10 m at about 1:50 (1 px = 12.5 mm)

A kitchen extension: two rooms, a door with its swing, two windows, a column, a straight stair of sixteen risers with its break line and UP arrow (ochre: it breaks the riser rule), an elevator, a ramp, a dimension chain, a label, a sofa, a pendant light, the selected partition with its grips, a crossing marquee, the level above as an underlay, the Wall tool drawing from the corner with an endpoint snap on a tracking ray, and Otto's halo and tag on the 3 m wall it just drew, with a pending deletion dashed round the column.

Every stroke keeps its screen width at any zoom (the renderer divides by the camera scale); only the underlay's wall bands, the light glyph and the label text are in millimetres. Text: room names 12 px Alegreya Sans, areas and every figure 11 px IBM Plex Mono. The halo is 34 px of mulberry at 22 %; the selection 2 px of terracotta at 100 % with a 12 % fill; the marquee 1 px at 8 %, dashed because it was dragged from right to left.

(e) Exports · the plan on an A3 sheet at 1:50, with the proposed title block

White sheet (it prints on real paper), 10 mm margin, pens in millimetres on paper whatever the scale: walls 0.50, connector outlines and the break line 0.35, jambs and leaves 0.25, treads and furniture 0.18, dimensions, swings and room outlines 0.13, the lift door 0.70. DXF · layers and AutoCAD colour indices

One layer per drawing layer (an optional prefix such as A-), lineweights from the pens, dashes as line types, poché as hatches. The ACI table has no sage, umber, indigo or ochre: these are its nearest fixed colours; 7 is black on a white background and white on a black one.

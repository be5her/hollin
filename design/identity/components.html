Hollin. components

Every shared control and panel of the app in the skin, light on paper (left) and dark on the desk (right). The two columns are one markup rendered under :root and under .dark, exactly as styles.css does it, so the hex values in the stylesheet are the single source. States that need a pointer (hover, active, focus) are shown statically, side by side. Four full-size mocks (1512 × 816 CSS px, the smoke window, shown at the reference 1890 × 1020) close the page. The written spec is 02-components.md.

Palette and type

The interface variables, read back from the stylesheet so they cannot drift. The plan and scene variables are the canvas document's (04-canvas.md); they are in the stylesheet here so the thumbnails and plan mocks draw in the right colours.

Catalog cards and category chips

The segmented Furniture / Materials strip, a small search with the Import… button, the category chips, then a two-column grid of 8 px paper cards: a square picture on the surface tint, the name at 12 px, the caption (category, size) in mono graphite. Context menus and toasts

A menu is a small sheet on the surface tint with an edge and the shadow, 8 px radius: 12 px rows, 28 px tall, shortcuts in mono graphite, the hovered or focused row on the elevated tint, danger rows in red, a check gutter when any row is a toggle. Scrollbars, text selection, focus rings

Thin scrollbars: an 8 px gutter, a graphite thumb at 35 % (55 % under the pointer), no track. Text selection is terracotta at 30 %. Focus-visible is one 2 px terracotta outline 1 px outside the control everywhere a text field's ring does not apply; the ring never shows for a pointer click.

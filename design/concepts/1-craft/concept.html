Type

Three open-licence families, all bundled as variable or static OFL files. Alegreya Sans is the working face: a humanist sans with calligraphic roots that still sets a dense panel cleanly. IBM Plex Mono carries every number: typewriter DNA, tabular by nature, with a slashed zero. Fraunces is used sparingly, for the wordmark, project names and the few headings that get to speak (Home, empty moments, About).

Palette

Every semantic variable in styles.css, both themes. Light is paper on a desk; dark is the same desk after the lamp goes on: warm near-black, never blue-grey. On the canvas the roles keep their own hues: selection and the UI accent are terracotta (red-orange), warning is ochre (yellow), snap is moss (green), dimensions are indigo ink (blue), and the room wash is a pale sage that only ever appears at low alpha, so a line in any of the four full-strength colours sits apart from it.

The palette on the canvas and in 3D

The plan as a drawing: graphite walls with an ink edge, a sage wash in the rooms, indigo dimensions, terracotta for what you touched. In 3D the solids are plaster, sand and timber; glass is the one cool note; the elevator car carries the accent.

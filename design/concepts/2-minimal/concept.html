Visual identity · concept 2 of 4

Minimal

Paper, ink and one vermilion mark. The interface is white with hairlines instead of filled panels, square corners, and a true-black night mode. The plan is drawn in black ink; colour appears only on what is alive: the thing you selected, the snap under your cursor, a warning. One accent, used once at a time.

One family in two cuts, drawn to the same skeleton, so a number set in the mono sits on the same line as its label in the sans without looking pasted in. Figures are tabular everywhere a column of them appears. Two weights only: 400 and 500. Nothing is bold.

Palette · every variable in styles.css

Six greys and the mark. Walls, dimensions, connectors and grid are ink; rooms are a grey wash. Selection is vermilion, snap is green, warnings amber, the underlay a pale grey: four live colours on an otherwise monochrome drawing, none of which can be confused with another or with the ink. The night palette is not an inversion: the ink becomes paper-white on true black, the greys are redrawn, and the mark warms to #ff6a45 so it does not vibrate on black.

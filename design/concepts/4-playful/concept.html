The wordmark is Rubik ExtraBold, lowercase, tracked to −3.5 %: the round bowls of c, u, b, b, y repeat the icon's radii. It is always ink (fg) in the chrome and sits beside the mark; the one decorated form, teal with a coral full stop, is reserved for About and the first-launch screen.

Type

Rubik (OFL, variable 300–900) for everything you read; Spline Sans Mono (OFL, variable 300–700) for everything you measure. Both ship as one variable file each, bundled under font-src 'self'. Rubik's slightly rounded corners carry the mood at 13 px without ever looking bubbly; Spline Sans Mono has the same soft terminals but a strict grid, tabular figures and a slashed zero, so 3000 mm still reads as exact.

Palette

Every semantic variable in styles.css, both themes. Light is cream, not white; dark is warm charcoal, not black. Teal is the accent and the selection: it is what you do. Coral is the home: room fills, cards, the illustrations, and the assistant. Blueberry is the number: dimensions. Snap is a leaf green, warning an amber, danger a red: five canvas hues at five distinct angles (teal 174°, coral 18°, blueberry 238°, leaf 88°, amber 40°), so nothing on the plan is ever mistaken for anything else.

Home, light

Cards become little homes: a project with a thumbnail shows its ground floor on cream in the plan colours; one without shows a coral plan doodle with the project's initials in a round badge, never a random gradient.

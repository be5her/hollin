# Building

The building of a project: its levels (floors, basements, mezzanines) and the vertical connectors between them (stairs, ladders, ramps, elevators, escalators).

- **Levels** have a name, a floor-to-floor height and a slab thickness. **Elevations are derived, never stored:** the ground level sits at the building's datum (0 by default); levels stacked above it add up the heights below them; basements hang below it, each its own height lower (negative elevations); mezzanines and split levels sit at an offset from a stacked host level. So changing a level's height moves every level above it, a deeper basement pushes the ones below it down, and grade stays put. Setting a level's elevation changes the value that produces it: the datum for the ground floor (the whole building moves), the height of the level below for a level above ground, its own height for a basement, its offset for a mezzanine.
- Levels are added on top, at the bottom, or above/below another level; duplicated (the copy, with new ids and its references remapped, is stacked right above); reordered by dragging; removed (levels above drop by its height; stairs and ramps ending on it go; elevators skip it; columns and shafts that reached up to it stop at their own level). A level that hosts a mezzanine cannot be removed until the mezzanine is moved or removed. A mezzanine can be moved to another stacked level (`hostLevelId`): it keeps its elevation when the new host reaches it, otherwise it sits on the new host's floor. `levelRemovalImpact` (core) says what a removal would take with it, grouped, without changing anything.
- **Connectors** belong to the building, not to a level: a stair (straight, L, U, winder, spiral or curved, along an axis path, with riser and tread intent), ladder, ramp (with a slope limit), elevator (shaft and car size, the levels it stops at and the door side at each), escalator. In this milestone they are data; their geometry, plan symbols, slab openings and rule warnings come with milestone 5. A connector's top must be above its bottom.
- **The project tab** shows the levels from top to bottom with their elevations in display units, the active level's name, elevation, height and slab (typed in any unit: `3.6m`, `3600`, `11' 10"`), its layers (show/hide, lock, opacity), and a section through the whole building with every level, the ground line and the connectors. Click a level, in the list or the section, to make it active. A level's name is edited in place (double-click it, or F2 on the focused row; Enter keeps, Escape drops). Its right-click menu adds a level above or below (asking for name, height and slab), a mezzanine (name, offset above the floor, height, slab), moves a mezzanine to another level (also the **Placed from** select in the details), and removes the level after listing what goes with it: everything on it by type, the stairs and ramps that end there, the elevators that stop skipping it, and spans cut short; a level that hosts a mezzanine says which to move first.

## Storage

Levels, layers and connectors live in `project.json`; each level's entities in its own level file (see the library README). The active level and the open right panel are view state, stored per machine in `.local/ui.json`.

## Agents

All allowed; every change is an undoable transaction attributed to the agent.

- `building.get`: levels bottom to top with derived elevation, height, top, slab, whether it is the ground or a basement, its layers and entity counts; the connectors.
- `building.level.add` (with `position` or `mezzanineOf`), `building.level.update` (name, height, slab, elevation, ground, `hostLevelId` for a mezzanine), `building.level.duplicate`, `building.level.move`, `building.level.remove`, and the read-only `building.level.impact` (what a removal would do).
- `building.connector.add`, `building.connector.update`, `building.connector.remove`.
- All of these can be steps of `plan.apply`.

# Plan

Editing on a level: layers and entities, and batches.

- **Layers** belong to a level and have a kind: structure (walls, openings, columns, slabs, voids, shafts), interior (rooms, fixtures, electrical and plumbing points) or decor (furniture items, lights). Dimensions and labels go on any layer. Every level starts with one of each; more can be added. Layers can be shown or hidden, locked, faded (opacity) and reordered. Moving the opacity slider makes many small changes that merge into one undo step.
- **Entities** are validated against their schema (`packages/core/src/doc/schema.ts`) on every change, with lengths rounded to 0.001 mm and defaults filled in. Their geometry is the `Path`: vertices joined by lines, arcs (by bulge) or cubic Béziers, with fillet or chamfer corners stored as parameters. An opening sits in a wall on the same level, at a distance along its path; deleting the wall deletes its openings. Columns, shafts and voids can span several levels (`span.topLevelId`) and then show on every level they pass through, mezzanines included.
- **The plan view** is the main view of the project tab (a Plan | Section switcher sits above it): the active level drawn from above on a canvas by `@studio/viewport`'s `plan2d` (see `packages/viewport/README.md`), with an adaptive grid in display units. The toolbar switches the level (also PageUp/PageDown with the canvas focused; the levels panel and the section follow the same choice), shows the level below or above faintly as a reference, and zooms (wheel at the cursor, buttons, Home or the fit button). Middle-drag or Space+drag pans. The footer shows the cursor in display units. Columns, rooms (a badge at the seed, or the drawn region), slabs, voids, dimensions and labels are drawn from their data; an agent's change appears on the canvas at once.
- **Walls** are drawn joined: each wall's body is derived from its reference line (`path`), thickness and alignment (the body centred on the line, or to its left or right), exactly for straight and arc walls, and the bodies of a level are united (Clipper2) together with corner fills where wall ends meet, so L corners close to their miter at any angle and thickness mix, T junctions and crossings merge, and curved walls keep their arcs. A wall is one entity per segment unless drawn as a single wall, which is how a run gets fillet or chamfer corners. Nothing of this is stored: `packages/core/src/geometry/walls.ts` derives it, and the plan view caches it by object identity (see `packages/viewport/README.md`).
- **The Wall tool** (`W`, or the toolbar): click the points of a run; every click adds a wall from the last point and shows the length while drawing; `A` makes the next segment an arc (click its end, then a point it passes through); clicking the first point closes the run; Enter ends it; Escape drops what is unfinished, then leaves the tool (`V` selects). The options bar sets thickness and height in any unit, which side of the line the body lies on, and whether the run becomes one entity.
- **Selecting and editing** (`V`, the default tool): click an entity to select it (the smallest thing under the pointer wins: a door before its wall, a wall before the room around it), Shift+click to add or remove, click empty floor to clear, Ctrl+A for everything on the level. Drag from empty floor for a marquee: to the right it is a window (what lies fully inside), to the left a crossing (what it touches, dashed). Drag a selected entity to move the selection (Ctrl drags a copy); walls move joined with their neighbours in the preview and one command is sent on release. Arrow keys nudge by 100 mm (Shift: 1 m), merged into one undo step. Delete deletes; Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste at the cursor (a wall's openings come along, as one batch); Ctrl+D duplicates. `R` rotates: click the pivot, a reference point, then the new direction (Ctrl keeps a copy); `Shift+M` mirrors across two clicked points. Right-click opens a menu (duplicate, delete, select all, zoom to fit). Escape cancels what is in progress, then clears the selection. Entities on hidden layers are not drawn or hit; locked layers are drawn but cannot be selected from the canvas (commands and agents can still change them). An entity that vanishes while selected (an agent's delete, an undo) leaves the selection, and a drag whose entity vanishes is dropped.
- **Grips and corners:** a single selected entity shows its grips: squares at the vertices of its path (a wall's reference line, a region's outline and holes) and circles at the middle of each segment. Drag a vertex to move it; the ends of other walls that meet it come along (Alt detaches), as one change. Drag a segment's middle to curve it through the cursor (Alt adds a vertex there instead); double-click it to straighten. Click a vertex to select the corner: the Properties panel then edits its position, its segment (straight, or an arc by radius and direction) and its corner (sharp, rounded by radius, chamfered by two distances); `F` and `C` round or chamfer it from the keyboard, Delete removes it, Escape goes back to the entity.
- **The Properties panel** (right column) shows the selected entity's fields in display units, typed in any unit (`30cm`, `11' 6"`): a wall's thickness, height (empty for the level's), which side of the line its body lies on, and its length; an opening's kind, width, height, sill, distance along the wall, hinge and swing side; a room's name and number; a label's text, height and rotation; a column's size and rotation; a slab's thickness; a dimension's offset; every entity's name. A field that is not a length turns red while typing and is put back on Enter. Several entities of one kind edit together (one change). Every edit carries the change number the panel saw, so an edit on stale data is refused and the panel shows who changed the entity meanwhile.
- **Stale edits:** edits addressed by index (a vertex, a field from a copy) may carry `baseSeq`, the change number the caller's copy had, and `epoch`; if the entity changed since, the command fails with `CONFLICT`, naming who changed it (you, the assistant, or an MCP agent by name), and nothing moves. The editor sends them with every grip and inspector edit.
- **Snapping:** while drawing, dragging or placing points, the cursor snaps within about 10 px to what is near, ranked endpoint, intersection, midpoint, centre (arcs, columns), perpendicular or tangent from the segment being drawn, the nearest point on a line, the extension of a line (with a dashed guide; two extensions crossing give an apparent intersection), an angle step from the last point, and finally the grid. Sources are wall reference lines and faces, opening jambs, column and region outlines and dimension lines. The footer and a marker on the canvas say what the cursor snapped to (a square for an endpoint, a triangle for a midpoint, a circle for a centre, a cross for an intersection, a right angle for a perpendicular). Alt held suspends snapping; Shift keeps the direction from the last point to 45° steps. The magnet in the toolbar switches snapping off and on, and its menu toggles each kind, angle steps (5° to 45°), the grid's visibility and the grid snap spacing (typed in any unit). These settings, and the Wall tool's thickness and height, are kept in the global settings (`editor` in `config.json`), so they follow the user across projects.
- **Exact input:** while a tool waits for a point (drawing a wall, moving or reshaping a selection, placing a mirror axis), typing a digit (or `@`, `-`, `.`, `+`) opens an input at the cursor. Enter takes a length along the current direction from the last point (`3000`, `3.5m`, `11'6"`), a polar coordinate (`3000<45`), a relative offset (`@1000,500`) or an absolute position (`1000,500`); while rotating, an angle in degrees. Escape closes it; text that is not a coordinate is refused and stays to be corrected. The length and angle of the segment being drawn are shown next to it. Lengths and angles elsewhere are typed in the tool options and the Properties panel.
- **Doors and windows** (`D`, `N`): hover a wall and the opening previews where the cursor projects on it, moved into the nearest gap between the wall's ends and its other openings (red when the wall has no room); click to place it, or type a distance from the wall's start (negative: from its end). For doors, `F` flips the swing side and `H` the hinge while placing. Sizes come from the options bar and are remembered in the settings (door 900 × 2100, window 1200 × 1200 with a 900 sill by default). The opening is cut out of the drawn wall; a door shows its leaf and swing, a window its glazing lines. Click an opening to select it before its wall; drag it to slide along its wall (over another wall, it moves there); a door's hinge and swing grips, the right-click menu and the Properties panel flip its swing; arrow keys slide it. An opening that no longer fits (its wall was shortened, or another opening overlaps it) is drawn in the warning colour; milestone 5 lists it as a rule warning.
- **`plan.apply`** runs a list of editing commands as one transaction: all succeed or nothing changes, and it is one undo step. A step can name what it creates (`as: "w1"`) and later steps refer to it as `"$w1"`. `dryRun` checks without applying. Only editing commands can be steps; batches cannot be nested. A batch of 20 steps or more by an agent first keeps the project in an automatic snapshot.

## Storage

Entities live in their level's file (see the library README). The plan's camera, the reference underlay choice and the chosen main view are view state, stored per machine in the project's `.local/ui.json` (`camera`, `underlay`, `centerView`) next to the active level. Snapping, grid and tool defaults are global settings: the `editor` section of `config.json` (see `packages/core/README.md`), written through the hidden `config.update` like every setting.

## Agents

All allowed and undoable: `plan.query` (entities by level, layer, type or id), `plan.layer.add/update/move/remove`, `plan.wall.add` (one straight or arc wall from two points, a run of points as one wall per segment or a single wall, or a full path; thickness, height and alignment), `plan.opening.add` (a door, window or plain opening in a wall at `along` or at the point nearest to `at`, with sizes and swing; refused with the free ranges when it does not fit), `plan.transform` (move by a vector, rotate about a pivot, mirror across a line, optionally as copies; a moved opening slides along its wall, a mirror swaps a door's swing side; `nudge` merges quick repeats into one undo step), `plan.path.vertex.move/insert/remove`, `plan.path.segment.set` (straight, or an arc by bulge or through a point) and `plan.path.corner.set` (fillet, chamfer or sharp) on any entity's path (`which`: a wall's path or a region's outline, or a region's hole), `plan.entity.add/update/delete`, `plan.apply`. The path commands and `plan.entity.update` take `baseSeq` and `epoch` for the conflict check above. Agents send intent (add this wall, set this field); the change is built from the current document in the main process, never from a possibly stale copy.
